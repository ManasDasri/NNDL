Try the demo

Paste a commercial contract and the model will flag which of the six risk-relevant clause types it contains, show the passages that triggered each flag, and the phrases it relied on.

The model is the project's TextCNN baseline (contract-level F1 0.846 on the held-out test contracts). It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you paste leaves this page. The stronger Legal-BERT model is about 50 times larger, too big to run here. A research project, not legal advice.
